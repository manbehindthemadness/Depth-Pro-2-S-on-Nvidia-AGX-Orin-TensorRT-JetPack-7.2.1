These TensorRT engines are model derivatives of Apple Depth Pro 2-S.

Apple Machine Learning Research Model is licensed under the Apple Machine Learning Research Model License Agreement.

The included APPLE-MODEL.txt limits use and redistribution to Research Purposes as defined in the agreement. Keep the license and this attribution with redistributed engines.

This conversion uses the original trained weights, static batch-one shapes, a single-frame causal window, and Apple's FP16 range adjustments. It omits the focal-length estimator and compiles the graph for TensorRT mixed-precision execution on NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin. Inputs and outputs remain FP32.
